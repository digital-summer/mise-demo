Layout experiment, draft 2

A is the refined layout built from your notes. B and C are two new structural directions on the same content. Pick one of the three. D sits beside whichever you pick: it is the destination for the direct mail campaign, not a fourth candidate. All of them link to one shared directory page and the same vendor page.

A · Merged

Split hero with the search panel straddling the divider below it. Supplier of the month beside the most-enquired list, three category rails with longer descriptions and rules between them, then the eight-step start-here list. Closes on the curated shortlist block.

B · Opening timeline

The eight steps become the page. Dark hero carrying the sequence, sticky search bar, then one band per step: sticky step description on the left, a rail of the vendors who handle it on the right. Supplier of the month interrupts at step four. No category grid.

C · Workbench

No hero. A command bar, then a three-column working view: category nav on the left, a stream of vendor rows grouped under sticky category headers in the middle, most-enquired and the shortlist prompt on the right. Checklist compressed to a strip near the foot.

Direct mail destination

Not a candidate layout. A separate page that ships alongside A, B or C, with its own audience and its own vendor set.

D · Mailer

Where the printed card lands. Opens by name from the code on the mailer, then eight category sections in checklist order, three suppliers in each, every card carrying the same three fields so the three can be read across. Sticky jump nav, no hero search, no supplier of the month, no most-enquired. Twenty-four suppliers in total.
