About the supplier

Teck Seng has supplied commercial kitchens in Singapore since 1987, working out of a 12,000 sq ft showroom and workshop in Tuas. We stock new and reconditioned cooking equipment, refrigeration and stainless steel fabrication, and we service what we sell.

Around half our work is with businesses opening their first outlet. We will walk your floor plan with you before quoting, and we will tell you which items on your list you do not need yet. Reconditioned equipment carries the same six-month parts warranty as new.

Fabrication is done in-house, so custom benches, hoods and sinks are measured, built and installed by the same team. Typical turnaround from confirmed order to installation is three weeks.
